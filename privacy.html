Daily Workout privacy

Updated November 9, 2026

Daily Workout is a personal workout app for its configured owner. The public website contains exercise plans. Personal workout results, ratings, weights and notes are saved in browser storage on the device where they are entered.

Optional Google backup

Connecting Google verifies the configured owner's email address and allows access to Google Drive files created or used with this app. It does not request access to all Drive files. Choosing Sync now sends finished workout records to a private Google Sheet and can restore finished history to this browser. Unfinished workouts and practice sessions stay on the device. The app does not change the Sheet's sharing permissions.

Remembered connections

The remembered-connection backend is hosted on Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database. Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted before database storage. The browser stores a revocable app session; the backend uses Google credentials to renew access and relay requested Drive and Sheets operations. App sessions expire after at most 90 days, and Google may require reconnection earlier. Workout records pass through this backend during sync; the backend does not store a separate workout-history database. Request logging is disabled in the app's Worker configuration.

Providers and control

GitHub Pages hosts the website, Google hosts account authorization and the private Sheet, and Cloudflare hosts the connection backend. These providers may process service information under their own policies. The app does not include advertising or analytics trackers and does not sell workout data.

Disconnect Google removes this browser's backend connection. It does not delete workout history. You can revoke the app's Google grant in your Google Account connections, delete the private Sheet in Google Drive, and clear this website's browser storage to remove local records. Export a local backup before clearing storage if you want to keep those records. Separate browsers and Home Screen apps may have separate local storage and connections.
