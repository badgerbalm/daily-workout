Daily Workout Privacy Policy

Updated November 18, 2026

Daily Workout is a personal-use workout application. The public website hosts workout prescriptions, exercise descriptions and images. It is not intended to publish personal workout or Oura records.

Workout information

Workout progress, completed exercises, entered treadmill and strength results, ratings, discomfort notes and drafts are saved in browser storage on the device where they are entered. When Google backup is connected, the current shared workout state is sent through the application backend and stored encrypted in Cloudflare D1. Finished daily workouts and review details are automatically backed up to the existing private Google Sheet when connected. Practice sessions are not included in that history backup.

Optional Google connection and backup

Google sign-in verifies the configured account and grants the app access to files created or used with this app. The app does not request access to all Google Drive files or change the Sheet’s sharing settings. Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted before storage in Cloudflare D1. The browser stores a revocable app session. Workout records pass through the backend during saves and Sheet sync.

Optional Oura connection

If you connect Oura, Daily Workout requests only the daily scope for daily summaries. The backend retrieves Daily Sleep and Daily Readiness scores, their dates and available contributors. Oura access and rotating refresh tokens, and the retrieved score records, are encrypted in Cloudflare D1. Oura update notifications are verified by the backend and used to refresh the records.

Daily sleep and readiness scores, score contributors, sleep-session duration and available continuity details are also written to an “Oura Daily Scores” tab in the existing private workout Google Sheet. The app does not encrypt cells in Google Sheets or change the Sheet’s sharing settings. A ChatGPT task can access this information only if its connected Google Sheets access and task instructions allow it; the app does not grant that access automatically. Oura data may be unavailable or delayed until the Oura app syncs the ring, and each row includes the Oura date so older data can be recognized.

Optional backup problem notifications

If enabled, the app stores your web push subscription encrypted in Cloudflare D1 and sends a generic notification when a backup needs attention. Notifications do not include workout results, Oura scores or health notes. On iPhone and iPad, web push requires adding the app to the Home Screen and granting notification permission.

Service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the public website. Cloudflare hosts the connection backend and D1 database. Google provides account authorization and the private Sheet. Oura provides authorization, daily summaries and update webhooks. OpenAI processes information that your connected ChatGPT task reads from the Sheet. These providers process data under their own policies. The app does not include advertising or analytics trackers and does not sell personal workout or Oura data.

Your choices and data removal

You can disconnect Google or Oura in the app and revoke their grants in the respective account settings. Disconnecting Oura removes its stored tokens, webhook records and score copies from the app’s Cloudflare database. It attempts to cancel Oura webhook subscriptions. Score rows already copied to Google Sheets remain there until you remove them from that Sheet. Disconnecting Google does not delete workout history or Sheet records. Clearing browser storage removes local records from that browser; export a local backup first if you want to keep them.
